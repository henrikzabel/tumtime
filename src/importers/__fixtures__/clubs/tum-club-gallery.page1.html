    TUM Student Club Gallery

    There are over 250 student clubs at TUM - find your community and discover campus life!

    Here you will find a complete overview of our student clubs at TUM - sorted by topic and location. Filter according to your interests or the campus that suits you best to quickly find the club that suits you and your passions.

    Did not find what you are looking for? We are happy to help you start a new club. Whether you just have an idea or have already gathered fellow students for your project, feel free to reach out to us!
